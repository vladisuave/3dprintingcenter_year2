Our Lab Rules [~ 3 min] Review this slide each week prior to the lesson content

Safety: hands off printers. The printer has a hot nozzle and moving parts that can pinch. The printer will keep moving, even if something is in its path.

Kind words and suggestions: everyone's design is their own artistic vision. Helpful ideas are welcome ('Have you tried...?', 'I like how...'), but we do not put down others designs.

Questions at the end: many of your questions will be answered in the lesson material. We answer any questions you still have at the end, this allows us to have more build time.

White filament: every print comes out white. Painting can be done at home or in week 6.

Stay on task: if you are off task you may be asked to go back to your classroom.

Locked items stay locked: some parts are locked on purpose so they stay the right size. Do not click the padlock to unlock them. If something will not move, it is supposed to stay put.

Tinkercad Controls [~6min] Print and place in stand up

DEMO LIVE on the projector while students WATCH. Chromebooks stay closed until after questions.

Their first 2 minutes of build time are a warm-up: move, lift and turn one letter.

Basic functions

Move: click and drag a shape (X and Y)

Lift: the black cone on top moves it up and down (Z).

Turn: the curved arrows.

Resize: the white squares. Also called handles.

Spin view: hold Ctrl and drag on empty space to orbit around your design. Dragging the view cube in the top-left corner also works.

Zoom: the + and – buttons on the left. A two-finger slide also works.

SUPER KEYS (hold the key down first, then click/drag/press):

Ctrl + drag: spin view. This is used a lot and worth repeating.

Ctrl + Shift + drag: move around the screen (pan) without spinning.

L: line things up. Select shapes, press L, then click a dot.

Shift + click: select more than one shape. This is needed for grouping.

Shift + drag a corner: resize evenly, no stretching.

Ctrl + Z: undo.

Ctrl + D: duplicate a shape (double letters like ANNA, or a stamp from the kit).

Ctrl + G: group.

Delete: select and click the trash can. Backspace also works.

Today's Build: 6 Steps [~2 min]

The Workplane already has the whole alphabet and the red plate with its ring.

1. Find the letters in your name and drag them onto the red plate, in order.

2. Double letters (like ANNA): select the letter and press Ctrl + D to make a copy.

3. Delete all the letters you did not use.

4. Line up: select the plate and all your letters, press L, and click ONLY the middle dot on the front-to-back line. The letters line up in a straight row across the middle of the plate.

5. Group it: lasso everything and press Ctrl + G.

6. Rename the project: click the project name at the top left and type your name, ready, keychain. This tells the adults it is ready to print. Example: Stasey ready keychain.

Line Up and Group [~3 min]

Optional LIVE DEMO. Line up first, then group.

1. Select: click on empty space and drag the orange loop around the plate and every letter. Shift + clicking each piece also works. Including the plate centers the row on the plate.

2. Press L. Dots appear on lines around the selection. Click ONLY the middle dot on the front-to-back (Y) line. The letters snap into a straight row.

Do NOT click the middle dot on the side-to-side (X) line. That slides every letter to the same spot and stacks them in a pile.

Fix: Ctrl + Z.

3. Press Ctrl + G or click the Group button. Test it: drag the plate. If every letter moves with it, it is grouped.

Ungroup if needed: Ctrl + Shift + G.

Note: Tinkercad turns a group into one color. Changing letters to the same color does not group them.

Long Name? Try These Tricks [BACKUP SLIDE]

Put up for individuals as needed or place in stand ups on tables.

Squeeze closer: slide letters so they overlap a little. On the plate, overlapping letters still print as one piece.

Shrink evenly: lasso the letters, hold Shift and drag a corner square. Keep letters at least about 8 mm tall so they are readable.

Nicknames or initials are always okay.

After any change, line the letters up again: L, middle front-to-back dot.
